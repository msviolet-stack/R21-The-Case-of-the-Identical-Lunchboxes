CASE BRIEFING

A Meal at Sarah’s Door

Sarah was found dead in her apartment at about 11.35 p.m. Earlier that evening, her neighbour John had delivered a glass lunchbox of food. A second similar lunchbox, a strand of red hair and evidence of an earlier injury led investigators towards several people.

The police identified four suspects: John, Amy, Adam and Lincoln. Your task is to establish who knowingly cooperated in delivering the harmful meal, rather than relying on an argument, hair colour or old contact.

Your task: Select the two people who knowingly participated and identify who handed over the lunchbox and who delivered it to Sarah.

Investigation rules

Exactly two of the four suspects knowingly cooperated in the harmful delivery. There were no other accomplices. The confirmed recordings, identities, timestamps and verified messages are accurate. A verified message is one personally sent by its named sender, without impersonation.

For the grid, match each suspect to a different location and associated object at 10.50 p.m. Each location and object must be used once. The object column records who was holding or using it at that time.

The supplied medical finding is part of this fictional case. No chemical, preparation method or symptom inference is needed to solve it. Written explanations should be discussed with the teacher.

The Four Suspects

Both John and Amy have red hair. Neither hair colour nor a friendly or hostile relationship establishes knowledge or intent.

Teacher Solution

Teacher Case Solution

Amy and John. Amy supplied the identifiable lunchbox; John knowingly delivered it to Sarah.

The numbered seal and blue corner clip establish one unbroken food chain. The confirmed medical finding links that food to Sarah’s death. The verified agreement shows that both Amy and John knew the meal was harmful before John delivered it. This is what rules out an innocent-delivery explanation.

Adam’s location and lack of involvement are supplied explicitly. Lincoln’s earlier recorded assistance explains the scratches and contact, while his cinema record places him elsewhere during the delivery. A red hair shared in appearance by two suspects cannot identify its source or establish intent.

The original claimed substance has been replaced with a non-specific harmful contaminant. The game makes no claim that a particular substance caused immediate death, immobility or the absence of a struggle.
