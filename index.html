A Mix-Up at Sarah’s Door

Teacher Solution

Teacher Case Solution

Amy and John. Amy supplied the look-alike lunchbox. John knowingly delivered it to Sarah.

The courtyard recording shows Amy giving John the blue-clipped box with seal 27. The corridor footage shows John delivering that same box to Sarah. Their verified messages establish that they both agreed to the switch before the delivery. Sarah’s original box was recovered with the quiz envelope intact.

Adam’s work call and Lincoln’s cinema record place them elsewhere at 6.50 p.m. The red hair and Lincoln’s earlier photo with Sarah do not identify either role.
